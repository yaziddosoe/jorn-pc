Quick answers

Before you ask

How long does a custom build take?+
Ready-built PCs in our lineup ship fast. Bespoke builds are typically done in 5–10 working days including the 24-hour burn-in test.
Do PCs ship fully built and tested?+
Yes. Every rig is assembled, cable-managed, benchmarked and stress-tested before it leaves. It arrives ready to plug in and play.
Can I upgrade parts later?+
Absolutely. If you bought the PC from us, bring it back any time — we'll upgrade storage, RAM, GPU or cooling and re-test the whole machine.
What does the 2-year warranty cover?+
Parts and labour for any hardware failure, plus free diagnostics for the life of the machine. Damage from misuse or physical trauma is not covered.
