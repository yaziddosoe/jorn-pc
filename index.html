Good questions

Quick answers before you ask

How long does a custom build take? +
Most builds ship within 7–10 days. Fully custom or water-cooled projects can take 2–3 weeks because every rig goes through a 24-hour burn-in and stress test before it leaves the bench.
Do you ship outside your city? +
Yes. We ship safely-packed rigs across the whole country and field-test the packaging on every generation before release — expansion foam, double boxes, the works.
What warranty do builds come with? +
Every build includes 2-year warranty on labour and workmanship, plus full manufacturer warranties on every component (usually 3–5 years). We also offer lifetime support on the machines we've built.
Can you upgrade my existing PC? +
Absolutely — and that's usually the smartest money you'll spend. Free consultation to figure out which single upgrade will bring the most frames per dollar, then we install and test it.
Do you take old parts as trade-in? +
Yes. Clean, working parts are tested and credited against your new build. If a part isn't worth anything we'll tell you straight, so you can keep it or recycle it sensibly.
